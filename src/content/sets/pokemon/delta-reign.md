## What's in Delta Reign

Mega Rayquaza ex is the headline card, with Mega Golisopod ex, Mega Golurk ex and Mega Malamar ex alongside it. The set also brings in Legendary Stadiums, pairs of Stadium cards whose art joins into one picture.

The Elite Trainer Box has 9 packs and an exclusive Kyogre illustration rare promo you can't pull from packs. Prerelease events run from 24 October to 1 November, and around ten NZ stores are selling prerelease tickets.

## Pre-orders in NZ

Most NZ stores that listed pre-orders have already sold out (see the table above). Some, like Otakumart, are keeping stock to sell in-store on release day instead of taking pre-orders, so check store sites and socials on 6 November. Singles usually start listing within a few days of release.
